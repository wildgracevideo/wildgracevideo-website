  problemText: Snooze was hosting a National Leadership Conference in Las Vegas
    for all of their Head Chefs and General Managers, and the new CEO needed a
    way to bring out a positive energy of the crowd with a video that felt
    authentic, high energy, and not "corporate". They hired me for this job
    because they had worked with me once before and knew that I was able to
    deliver a high quality product with a small and flexible team.
  processText: We traveled to 6 Snooze locations, within 5 different states, all
    within the span of 10 days to capture all the footage we needed. Since the
    turnaround for this video was very quick, I was strategizing and helping
    their team storyboard the video as the shoots were happening. I helped them
    shift their idea of having a 7-minute long full hype video to shifting the
    video into a more calculated story arc format. I recommended we start with
    building trust with the audience through recognizing the hard work and grit
    that it takes to be in this industry, showcasing a moment of transformation,
    and ending the video with full hype to get the audience excited for what was
    to come.
  resultText: After showcasing this video, the marketing directors were flooded
    with texts from chefs and GM's saying how much it moved them and how much
    the video captured the real essence of Snooze. The video was shared amongst
    friends and coworkers from all areas of the company, and it solidified the
    positive culture that Snooze strives for.
